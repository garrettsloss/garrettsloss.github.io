# U trgovini 🛒🇭🇷

*At the Grocery Store*

## Croatian story 🇭🇷

Ana danas ide u trgovinu. 😊 Treba nekoliko stvari za kuću.

U trgovini Ana uzima kruh, mlijeko i jabuke. Gleda jabuke. Crvene su i izgledaju dobro. 🍎

Jedan čovjek također gleda jabuke.

„Oprostite, gdje je voda?” pita čovjek.

„Voda je tamo,” kaže Ana i pokazuje rukom.

„Hvala!” kaže čovjek.

Ana još uzima vodu i sir. Sada ima kruh, mlijeko, jabuke, vodu i sir.

Ide do blagajne. Tamo je ljubazna blagajnica.

„Dobar dan,” kaže blagajnica.

„Dobar dan,” kaže Ana.

Blagajnica skenira proizvode.

„To je deset eura,” kaže blagajnica.

Ana plaća i uzima vrećicu. 🛍️

„Hvala. Doviđenja!”

„Doviđenja!” kaže blagajnica.

Ana ide kući. Kod kuće je sretna. Ima sve što treba. 😊

## English translation 🇬🇧

Ana goes to the grocery store today. 😊 She needs a few things for the house.

In the store, Ana takes bread, milk, and apples. She looks at the apples. They are red and look good. 🍎

A man is also looking at the apples.

“Excuse me, where is the water?” asks the man.

“The water is over there,” says Ana and points with her hand.

“Thank you!” says the man.

Ana also takes water and cheese. Now she has bread, milk, apples, water, and cheese.

She goes to the checkout. There is a friendly cashier there.

“Good day,” says the cashier.

“Good day,” says Ana.

The cashier scans the products.

“That is ten euros,” says the cashier.

Ana pays and takes a bag. 🛍️

“Thank you. Goodbye!”

“Goodbye!” says the cashier.

Ana goes home. At home she is happy. She has everything she needs. 😊

## Useful Croatian patterns 🧩

### 1. Treba mi / Treba...

**Meaning:** to need

Examples:

- Treba mi voda. = I need water.
- Treba mi kruh. = I need bread.
- Ana treba nekoliko stvari. = Ana needs a few things.

This is one of the most useful everyday Croatian patterns.

### 2. Gdje je...?

**Meaning:** Where is...?

Examples:

- Gdje je voda? = Where is the water?
- Gdje je trgovina? = Where is the store?
- Gdje je sir? = Where is the cheese?

Use it whenever you need help finding something.

### 3. Tamo

**Meaning:** there / over there

Examples:

- Voda je tamo. = The water is there.
- Moj prijatelj je tamo. = My friend is over there.

A very common location word.

### 4. Dobar dan

**Meaning:** Good day / Hello

Examples:

- Dobar dan! = Hello!
- Dobar dan, Ana. = Hello, Ana.

A polite greeting used during the day.

### 5. Ima...

**Meaning:** has / there is-are

Examples:

- Ana ima vodu. = Ana has water.
- Ima kruh i sir. = There is bread and cheese / He or she has bread and cheese.

This verb (*imati*, to have) is extremely common.

## Quick comprehension check 🎯

### 1. Gdje Ana ide danas?

*(Where does Ana go today?)*

A. U školu.  
B. U trgovinu.  
C. U park.

### 2. Što Ana kupuje?

*(What does Ana buy?)*

A. Kruh, mlijeko, jabuke, vodu i sir.  
B. Knjige i olovke.  
C. Banane i sok.

### 3. Koliko koštaju proizvodi?

*(How much do the products cost?)*

A. Pet eura.  
B. Deset eura.  
C. Dvadeset eura.

## Answer key ✅

1. **B. U trgovinu.**
2. **A. Kruh, mlijeko, jabuke, vodu i sir.**
3. **B. Deset eura.**
