# Dobro jutro! ☀️

*Good morning!*

## Croatian story 🇭🇷

Ana se budi. ☀️

" Dobro jutro!" kaže Ana.

Ana pije kavu. ☕

" Mm, kava je dobra," kaže Ana.

Marko se budi. 😴

" Dobro jutro, Marko," kaže Ana.

" Dobro jutro," kaže Marko.

Marko pije čaj. 🍵

" Čaj je dobar," kaže Marko.

Ana jede kruh. 🍞

" Kruh je dobar," kaže Ana.

Marko jede jabuku. 🍎

" Jabuka je dobra," kaže Marko.

Ana i Marko su zadovoljni. 😊

" Kava ili čaj?" pita Ana.

" Čaj, hvala," kaže Marko.

" Kruh ili jabuka?" pita Marko.

" Jabuka, hvala," kaže Ana.

Dobro jutro! 👋

## English translation 🇬🇧

Ana wakes up. ☀️

"Good morning!" says Ana.

Ana drinks coffee. ☕

"Mmm, the coffee is good," says Ana.

Marko wakes up. 😴

"Good morning, Marko," says Ana.

"Good morning," says Marko.

Marko drinks tea. 🍵

"The tea is good," says Marko.

Ana eats bread. 🍞

"The bread is good," says Ana.

Marko eats an apple. 🍎

"The apple is good," says Marko.

Ana and Marko are content. 😊

"Coffee or tea?" asks Ana.

"Tea, thanks," says Marko.

"Bread or an apple?" asks Marko.

"An apple, thanks," says Ana.

Good morning! 👋

## Useful Croatian patterns 🧩

**1. Dobro jutro!** = Good morning!

- *Dobro* = good, *jutro* = morning.
- Use this greeting in the morning. 😊

**2. Piti / pijem** = to drink / I drink

- *Ana pije kavu.* = Ana drinks coffee.
- *Ja pijem čaj.* = I drink tea.

**3. Jesti / jedem** = to eat / I eat

- *Marko jede jabuku.* = Marko eats an apple.
- *Ja jedem kruh.* = I eat bread.

**4. je dobar / je dobra** = is good

- *Kava je dobra.* = The coffee is good. (feminine noun → dobra)
- *Čaj je dobar.* = The tea is good. (masculine noun → dobar)
- Adjectives change form based on the noun's gender.

**5. ili** = or

- *Kava ili čaj?* = Coffee or tea?
- *Kruh ili jabuka?* = Bread or an apple?

2. **b) Jabuku** — *Marko jede jabuku.* (Marko eats an apple.)
3. **c) Zadovoljni** — *Ana i Marko su zadovoljni.* (Ana and Marko are content.)
