# 🇭🇷 Hrvatski za početnike

Croatian graded reader · A0–A1 · Story 1

## ☀️ Jedan lijep dan

A Beautiful Day

Read the Croatian story first. The emojis give you clues, so try to understand each sentence before looking at the English translation.

### 🇭🇷 Priča

Ana je kod kuće. 🏠

Ona je sretna jer je danas lijep dan. ☀️

Ana ustaje i ide u kuhinju. 🍳

Želi kavu i kruh. ☕🍞

Ona otvara hladnjak, ali nema mlijeka. 🥛❌

„Nema problema!” kaže Ana.

Oblači jaknu i ide u trgovinu. 🧥🛒

U trgovini vidi svog prijatelja Marka. 👋

„Bok, Ana! Kako si?” pita Marko.

„Dobro sam, hvala! A ti?”

„I ja sam dobro. Kamo ideš?”

„Idem kući. Želiš li kavu?”

„Da, hvala!” kaže Marko. ☕

Ana kupuje mlijeko i dvije jabuke. 🥛🍎🍎

Zatim Ana i Marko idu kući.

Piju kavu, jedu kruh i razgovaraju. ☕🥖💬

Ana je sretna. Ima kavu, hranu i dobrog prijatelja. ❤️

## 🇬🇧 English translation

### A Beautiful Day

Ana is at home. 🏠

She is happy because today is a beautiful day. ☀️

Ana gets up and goes to the kitchen. 🍳

She wants coffee and bread. ☕🍞

She opens the fridge, but there is no milk. 🥛❌

“No problem!” says Ana.

She puts on her jacket and goes to the shop. 🧥🛒

At the shop, she sees her friend Marko. 👋

“Hi, Ana! How are you?” asks Marko.

“I’m good, thanks! And you?”

“I’m good too. Where are you going?”

“I’m going home. Would you like some coffee?”

“Yes, thank you!” says Marko. ☕

Ana buys milk and two apples. 🥛🍎🍎

Then Ana and Marko go home.

They drink coffee, eat bread and talk. ☕🥖💬

Ana is happy. She has coffee, food and a good friend. ❤️

## 🧩 Useful Croatian patterns

These are some of the most useful structures hidden in the story. Learn the phrases as chunks rather than memorising every grammar rule at once.

1 · SAYING WHO OR HOW YOU ARE

Ja sam... / Ona je...

- Ja sam sretan. — I am happy. (male speaker)

- Ja sam sretna. — I am happy. (female speaker)

- Ona je kod kuće. — She is at home.

The verb biti (to be) changes: sam, si, je, smo, ste, su.

2 · PRESENT TENSE

Ana ide. / Ana kupuje.

- ide — goes / is going

- želi — wants

- vidi — sees

- kupuje — buys

- pije — drinks

- jede — eats

Croatian present-tense verbs often cover both “does” and “is doing.”

3 · NEGATION

Nema mlijeka. — There is no milk.

Nisam umoran. — I am not tired. (male speaker)

Nema is a very common way to say something is absent or unavailable.

4 · ASKING QUESTIONS

Kako si? — How are you?

Kamo ideš? — Where are you going?

Želiš li kavu? — Would you like coffee?

Adding li after a verb is a common way to form yes/no questions.

5 · CONNECTING IDEAS

- i — and

- ali — but

- jer — because

- zatim — then

- a ti? — and you?

These little words help you understand longer sentences without learning complicated grammar.
