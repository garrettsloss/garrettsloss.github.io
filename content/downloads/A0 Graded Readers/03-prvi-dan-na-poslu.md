# Prvi dan na poslu 💼🇭🇷
*First Day at Work*

## Croatian story 🇭🇷

Marko je na poslu. 💼 Danas je prvi dan.

Marko ide u ured. Ured je velik i lijep. 🏢

Ivana je u uredu.

"Dobro jutro!" kaže Marko.

"Dobro jutro! Ja sam Ivana, šefica," kaže Ivana. "Vi ste Marko?"

"Da, ja sam Marko."

"Drago mi je!" kaže Ivana. "Ovo je vaše mjesto."

"Hvala! Drago mi je!" kaže Marko. Marko sjeda.

Luka je kolega. Luka je u uredu.

"Bok, Marko! Ja sam Luka. Želiš li kavu?"

"Da, kavu, molim!" kaže Marko.

Luka donosi kavu. Marko pije kavu u uredu. ☕

"Hvala, Luka!" kaže Marko.

Marko je sretan. Prvi dan je dobar. 😊

## English translation 🇬🇧

Marko is at work. 💼 Today is the first day.

Marko goes to the office. The office is big and lovely. 🏢

Ivana is in the office.

"Good morning!" says Marko.

"Good morning! I am Ivana, the boss," says Ivana. "You are Marko?"

"Yes, I am Marko."

"Nice to meet you!" says Ivana. "This is your place."

"Thank you! Nice to meet you!" says Marko. Marko sits down.

Luka is a colleague. Luka is in the office.

"Hi, Marko! I am Luka. Would you like a coffee?"

"Yes, a coffee, please!" says Marko.

Luka brings the coffee. Marko drinks the coffee in the office. ☕

"Thank you, Luka!" says Marko.

Marko is happy. The first day is good. 😊

## Useful Croatian patterns 🧩

**1. Where you are: *u* + locative, *na poslu***
- Last story used **u** + accusative for movement (*Idem u grad*). To say where you already are, **u** takes the locative. For most masculine nouns the ending is **-u**.
- **ured → u uredu**, **grad → u gradu**, **park → u parku**, **kafić → u kafiću**
- **posao** (work, job) becomes **poslu**, and it uses **na**: *na poslu* (at work). Learn this one as a phrase.
- Examples: *Idem u ured.* (I'm going to the office.) *Ivana je u uredu.* (Ivana is in the office.) *Ana je u gradu.* (Ana is in town.)

**2. *Drago mi je!***
- Meaning: "Nice to meet you!" Literally "It is dear to me." It stays the same for men and women, and you say it back to the other person.
- Examples: *Ja sam Ana. Drago mi je!* (I'm Ana. Nice to meet you!) *Drago mi je, Marko.* (Nice to meet you, Marko.)

**3. Formal *vi* and informal *ti***
- With a boss or a stranger, use **vi**: *Vi ste Marko?* *Želite li kavu?* With a colleague or friend, use **ti**: *Ti si Marko?* *Želiš li kavu?*
- Endings: **vi** ends in **-te** (*želite, pijete*), **ti** ends in **-š** (*želiš, piješ*). "You are" is **vi ste** and **ti si**.
- Examples: *Što želite?* (formal) / *Što želiš?* (informal)

**4. Possessives agree with the noun: *vaše mjesto***
- **vaš** (m), **vaša** (f), **vaše** (n) all mean "your" (formal). The ending matches the noun, like adjectives do.
- Examples: *Ovo je vaš ured.* (This is your office.) *Ovo je vaša kava.* (This is your coffee.) *Ovo je vaše mjesto.* (This is your place.)

## Quick comprehension check 🎯

**1. Tko je Ivana? / Who is Ivana?**
- a) Konobarica / A waitress
- b) Šefica / The boss
- c) Prijateljica / A friend

**2. Što pije Marko? / What does Marko drink?**
- a) Kavu / Coffee
- b) Vodu / Water
- c) Sok / Juice

**3. Što znači "Drago mi je"? / What does "Drago mi je" mean?**
- a) Doviđenja / Goodbye
- b) Hvala / Thank you
- c) Nice to meet you / Nice to meet you

## Answer key

1. **b**: Ivana kaže: "Ja sam Ivana, šefica."
2. **a**: Marko pije kavu u uredu.
3. **c**: "Drago mi je" znači "Nice to meet you."
