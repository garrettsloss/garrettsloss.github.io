# Posjet prijatelju 🏠🇭🇷
*A Visit to a Friend*

## Croatian story 🇭🇷

Petar je prijatelj. Petar je u stanu. 🏠 Stan je velik i lijep.

Ana ide u stan.

"Bok!" kaže Ana.

"Bok! Dobro došla!" kaže Petar.

Ana donosi kolač. 🍰

"Izvoli!" kaže Ana. Ana daje kolač Petru.

"Hvala! Super!" kaže Petar. "Želiš li kavu?"

"Da, kavu, molim," kaže Ana.

Petar donosi kavu. Petar daje kavu Ani. "Izvoli!"

Petar i Ana sjede u stanu i piju kavu. ☕

"Je li kava dobra?" pita Petar.

"Da, kava je topla i dobra!" kaže Ana.

"Kolač je super!" kaže Petar.

Petar i Ana su sretni. Posjet je lijep. 😊

## English translation 🇬🇧

Petar is a friend. Petar is in the apartment. 🏠 The apartment is big and lovely.

Ana goes into the apartment.

"Hi!" says Ana.

"Hi! Welcome!" says Petar.

Ana brings a cake. 🍰

"Here you go!" says Ana. Ana gives the cake to Petar.

"Thank you! Great!" says Petar. "Would you like a coffee?"

"Yes, a coffee, please," says Ana.

Petar brings the coffee. Petar gives the coffee to Ana. "Here you go!"

Petar and Ana sit in the apartment and drink coffee. ☕

"Is the coffee good?" asks Petar.

"Yes, the coffee is warm and good!" says Ana.

"The cake is great!" says Petar.

Petar and Ana are happy. The visit is lovely. 😊

## Useful Croatian patterns 🧩

**1. Welcoming someone: *Dobro došla!***
- Meaning: "Welcome!" The second word agrees with the person arriving: **došao** (man), **došla** (woman), **došli** (several people).
- Examples: *Dobro došao!* (to a man) *Dobro došla!* (to a woman) *Dobro došli!* (to a group)

**2. Giving to someone: the dative case, *Petru*, *Ani***
- *Ana daje kolač Petru.* (Ana gives the cake to Petar.) The person who receives something is in the dative. The thing given stays in the accusative (*kolač, kavu*).
- Masculine names and nouns usually end in **-u**: **Petar → Petru**, **Marko → Marku**, **prijatelj → prijatelju**. Feminine nouns change **-a** to **-i**: **Ana → Ani**, **Marija → Mariji**, **prijateljica → prijateljici**.
- Examples: *Dajem kavu Ani.* (I give coffee to Ana.) *Donosim kolač prijatelju.* (I bring a cake to my friend.)

**3. Informal commands and questions: *Izvoli, Želiš li***
- Among friends, **izvoli** replaces **izvolite**, and **želiš** replaces **želite**. This is the same **ti** and **vi** difference from the work story.
- Examples: *Izvoli kavu!* (Here's your coffee!) *Želiš li kolač?* (Do you want cake?)

**4. Yes/no questions with *je li***
- With **je**, the question word is **Je li**: *Je li kava dobra?* (Is the coffee good?) With other verbs, **li** follows the verb: *Želiš li kavu?*
- Examples: *Je li kolač dobar?* (Is the cake good?) *Je li stan velik?* (Is the apartment big?)

**5. More than one person: *piju, sjede***
- One person: *Ana pije kavu. Petar sjedi.* Two or more: *Ana i Petar piju kavu. Ana i Petar sjede.*
- Endings differ by verb, so learn the plural with each verb: **pije → piju**, **sjedi → sjede**, **ide → idu**, **daje → daju**.
- Examples: *Petar i Ana piju kavu.* (Petar and Ana drink coffee.) *Piju kavu u stanu.* (They drink coffee in the apartment.)

## Quick comprehension check 🎯

**1. Što Ana daje Petru? / What does Ana give Petar?**
- a) Kavu / Coffee
- b) Kolač / Cake
- c) Kruh / Bread

**2. Što piju Ana i Petar? / What do Ana and Petar drink?**
- a) Vodu / Water
- b) Sok / Juice
- c) Kavu / Coffee

**3. Što znači "Dobro došla!"? / What does "Dobro došla!" mean?**
- a) Welcome! / Welcome!
- b) Good morning! / Good morning!
- c) Goodbye! / Goodbye!

## Answer key

1. **b**: Ana daje kolač Petru.
2. **c**: Petar i Ana piju kavu.
3. **a**: "Dobro došla!" znači "Welcome!"
