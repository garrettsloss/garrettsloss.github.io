# Kava u gradu ☕

*Coffee in the City*

## Croatian story 🇭🇷

Ana je u gradu. Ona je u kafiću. ☕

Ana vidi konobara. Konobar je mlad.

"Dobar dan!" kaže Ana.

"Dobar dan! Što želite?" pita konobar.

"Jednu kavu, molim."

"Izvolite. Želite li i kolač? 🍰"

Ana gleda kolače. Ima čokoladni kolač i kolač s jabukom. 🍎

"Imate li kolač s jabukom?" pita Ana.

"Da, imamo."

"Onda želim kolač s jabukom, molim."

Konobar donosi kavu i kolač. Ana pije kavu i jede kolač. Kolač je jako dobar! 😋

"Koliko je to?" pita Ana.

"Pet eura," kaže konobar.

Ana plaća. "Hvala! Doviđenja!"

"Doviđenja! Lijep dan!" 😊

## English translation 🇬🇧

Ana is in the city. She is in a café. ☕

Ana sees the waiter. The waiter is young.

"Good day!" says Ana.

"Good day! What would you like?" asks the waiter.

"One coffee, please."

"Here you are. Would you like a cake too? 🍰"

Ana looks at the cakes. There is a chocolate cake and an apple cake. 🍎

"Do you have apple cake?" asks Ana.

"Yes, we do."

"Then I would like the apple cake, please."

The waiter brings the coffee and the cake. Ana drinks the coffee and eats the cake. The cake is very good! 😋

"How much is that?" asks Ana.

"Five euros," says the waiter.

Ana pays. "Thank you! Goodbye!"

"Goodbye! Have a nice day!" 😊

## Useful Croatian patterns 🧩

**1. Što želite? / Želim ...** (What would you like? / I would like ...)

*Želite* is the polite "you" form of *željeti* (to want, wish). *Želim* is the "I" form. Using the polite *vi* form is standard with waiters, shopkeepers, and strangers.

- *Što želite?* = What would you like?
- *Želim kavu.* = I would like a coffee.
- *Želite li kolač?* = Would you like a cake?

**2. Question with li** (yes/no questions)

To make a yes/no question, put the verb first and add *li* after it.

- *Želite* → *Želite li kolač?* = Would you like a cake?
- *Imate* → *Imate li kolač s jabukom?* = Do you have apple cake?
- *Imaš* → *Imaš li kavu?* = Do you have coffee? (informal)

**3. Accusative case: kava → kavu, kolač → kolač**

When something is the object of the verb (the thing being wanted, eaten, or drunk), it appears in the accusative case. Feminine nouns ending in *-a* change to *-u*. Masculine inanimate nouns (things, not people or animals) stay the same.

- *kava* (nominative) → *Pije kavu.* = She drinks coffee.
- *kolač* (nominative) → *Jede kolač.* = She eats cake.
- *Želim kavu.* = I want a coffee.

Note that *jedan* also changes: *jedna kava* becomes *jednu kavu* in the accusative.

**4. s jabukom** (with apple)

The preposition *s* (with) is followed by the instrumental case. Feminine nouns ending in *-a* take *-om*.

- *jabuka* → *s jabukom* = with an
