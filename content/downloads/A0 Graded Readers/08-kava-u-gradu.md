# Kava u gradu ☕ 🇭🇷

**Coffee in the City**

---

## Priča 🇭🇷

Ja sam Marko. Ja sam u gradu. 🏙️

Danas je subota. Sunce je veliko i toplo. ☀️ Ja imam mali ruksak i telefon. 📱

Ja sam u kafiću. Kafić je malen, ali lijep. Tamo je Ana. Ana je konobarica. 😊

"Dobar dan!" kaže Ana. "Što želite?"

"Dobar dan! Ja želim kavu, molim," kažem ja.

"Želite li mlijeko?" pita Ana.

"Da, želim mlijeko, molim."

Ana ide u kuhinju. Ja čekam. Gledam ljude na ulici. Tamo je jedna žena sa psom. 🐕 Pas je velik i smiješan.

Ana dolazi. "Izvolite! Kava s mlijekom."

"Hvala! Koliko košta?" pitam ja.

"Dva eura," kaže Ana.

Ja imam novac. Plaćam kavu. 💶

"Hvala! Doviđenja!" kaže Ana.

"Doviđenja!" kažem ja. Kava je odlična! ☕

---

## English translation 🇬🇧

I am Marko. I am in the city. 🏙️

Today is Saturday. The sun is big and warm. ☀️ I have a small backpack and a phone. 📱

I am in a café. The café is small, but nice. Ana is there. Ana is a waitress. 😊

"Good day!" says Ana. "What would you like?"

"Good day! I would like a coffee, please," I say.

"Would you like milk?" asks Ana.

"Yes, I would like milk, please."

Ana goes to the kitchen. I wait. I look at the people on the street. There is a woman with a dog. 🐕 The dog is big and funny.

Ana comes. "Here you go! Coffee with milk."

"Thank you! How much does it cost?" I ask.

"Two euros," says Ana.

I have money. I pay for the coffee. 💶

"Thank you! Goodbye!" says Ana.

"Goodbye!" I say. The coffee is excellent! ☕

## Useful Croatian patterns 🧩

**1. Ja želim ... (I want / I would like ...)**

- *Ja želim kavu.* = I would like a coffee.
- *Ja želim mlijeko.* = I would like milk.

Note that *kava* (coffee) becomes *kavu* after *želim*. This is the **accusative case**, used for the thing you want, have, or buy. Feminine nouns ending in **-a** change to **-u**:

- *kava* → *kavu*
- *voda* (water) → *vodu*
- *torta* (cake) → *tortu*

Masculine and neuter nouns usually stay the same for now: *Želim sok* (I would like juice), *Želim mlijeko*.

**2. Molim (please / you're welcome / pardon)**  
A very common polite word. *Kavu, molim.* = A coffee, please. You can use it to order almost anything.

**3. Želite li ...? (Would you like ...?)**  
This is the polite "you" form of *želim*, with *li* added to make a yes/no question.

- *Želite li mlijeko?* = Would you like milk?
- *Želite li vodu?* = Would you like water?

Compare: *ja želim* (I want), *vi želite* (you want, polite).

**4. Koliko košta? (How much does it cost?)**  
A reusable question for shopping. *Koliko košta kava?* = How much does the coffee cost?

**5. Izvolite! (Here you go! / Please, go ahead)**  
Said when handing someone something or inviting them to take or enter. You will hear it constantly in shops and cafés.

## Quick comprehension check 🎯

**1. Tko je Ana? (Who is Ana?)**

- a) Ana je konobarica. (Ana is a waitress.)
- b) Ana je učiteljica. (Ana is a teacher.)
- c) Ana je Markova sestra. (Ana is Marko's sister.)

**2. Što Marko želi? (What does Marko want?)**

- a) Marko želi vodu. (Marko wants water.)
- b) Marko želi kavu s mlijekom. (Marko wants coffee with milk.)
- c) Marko želi sok. (Marko wants juice.)

**3. Koliko košta kava? (How much does the coffee cost?)**

- a) Jedan euro. (One euro.)
- b) Dva eura. (Two euros.)
- c) Pet eura. (Five euros.)
