# Vožnja autobusom 🚌🇭🇷
*A Bus Ride*

## Croatian story 🇭🇷

Ana je na stanici. 🚏 Dan je lijep. ☀️ Ana vidi autobus. 🚌

Ana ulazi u autobus.

"Dobar dan! Što želite?" kaže vozač.

"Dobar dan! Kartu, molim. Koliko je karta?" pita Ana.

"Dva eura," kaže vozač.

"Idem u grad," kaže Ana. Ana daje novac. "Izvolite!"

"Hvala! Izvolite kartu," kaže vozač.

"Hvala!" kaže Ana. Karta je mala i plava. 🎫

Ana vidi mjesto. Ana sjeda. 😊

Autobus ide. Ana gleda grad. Grad je velik i lijep. 🏙️ Ana vidi park. 🌳 Ana vidi kafić. ☕

"Izlazim!" kaže Ana.

"Doviđenja!" kaže Ana.

"Doviđenja!" kaže vozač.

Ana je u gradu. Ana je sretna.

## English translation 🇬🇧

Ana is at the bus stop. 🚏 The day is lovely. ☀️ Ana sees a bus. 🚌

Ana gets on the bus.

"Good day! What would you like?" says the driver.

"Good day! A ticket, please. How much is the ticket?" asks Ana.

"Two euros," says the driver.

"I'm going to town," says Ana. Ana gives the money. "Here you go!"

"Thank you! Here is your ticket," says the driver.

"Thank you!" says Ana. The ticket is small and blue. 🎫

Ana sees a seat. Ana sits down. 😊

The bus goes. Ana looks at the town. The town is big and lovely. 🏙️ Ana sees a park. 🌳 Ana sees a café. ☕

"I'm getting off!" says Ana.

"Goodbye!" says Ana.

"Goodbye!" says the driver.

Ana is in town. Ana is happy.

## Useful Croatian patterns 🧩

**1. Going somewhere: *u* + accusative, *Idem u grad.***
- Meaning: "I'm going to town."
- After **u** ("into, to"), a noun that names the destination of movement is in the accusative. Masculine nouns for things (**grad, park, ured, autobus**) stay the same. Feminine nouns change **-a** to **-u**.
- Examples: *Ana ulazi u autobus.* (Ana gets on the bus.) *Idem u kafić.* (I'm going to the café.) *Idem u školu.* (I'm going to school.)

**2. Present tense: *-m* for "I", *-e* or *-i* for "he/she"**
- **ići** (to go): *idem, ideš, ide*
- **izlaziti** (to get off, to go out): *izlazim, izlaziš, izlazi*
- **gledati** (to look at, to watch): *gledam, gledaš, gleda*
- Examples: *Idem u grad.* (I'm going to town.) *Ana ide u grad.* (Ana is going to town.) *Izlazim!* (I'm getting off!) *Ana gleda grad.* (Ana looks at the town.)

**3. Asking for something: *Kartu, molim.***
- The accusative **-u** from "Kavu, molim" works for any feminine noun: **karta → kartu**.
- Examples: *Kartu, molim.* (A ticket, please.) *Izvolite kartu.* (Here is your ticket.) *Vodu, molim.* (Water, please.)

**4. Greetings agree with their noun: *Dobar dan, Dobro jutro***
- **Dan** is masculine, so **dobar**. **Jutro** is neuter, so **dobro**. **Dobar dan** is the standard greeting during the day.
- Examples: *Dobar dan!* (Good day!) *Dobro jutro!* (Good morning!) *Dan je lijep.* (The day is lovely.) *Karta je mala.* (The ticket is small.)

## Quick comprehension check 🎯

**1. Kamo ide Ana? / Where is Ana going?**
- a) U park / To the park
- b) U grad / To town
- c) U kafić / To the café

**2. Koliko je karta? / How much is the ticket?**
- a) Pet eura / Five euros
- b) Tri eura / Three euros
- c) Dva eura / Two euros

**3. Što znači "Izlazim!"? / What does "Izlazim!" mean?**
- a) I'm getting off! / I'm getting off!
- b) I'm sitting down! / I'm sitting down!
- c) I'm going home! / I'm going home!

## Answer key

1. **b**: Ana kaže: "Idem u grad."
2. **c**: Vozač kaže: "Dva eura."
3. **a**: Ana izlazi iz autobusa u gradu.
