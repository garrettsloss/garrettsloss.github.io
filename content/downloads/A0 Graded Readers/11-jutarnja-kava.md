# Jutarnja kava ☕🇭🇷

*Morning Coffee*

## Croatian story 🇭🇷

Ja sam Ana. Ja sam u kafiću. ☕

Konobar kaže: "Dobro jutro! Što želite?"

Ana kaže: "Dobro jutro! Kavu, molim."

"Želite li mlijeko?" pita konobar.

"Da, mlijeko, molim," kaže Ana. "I kruh, molim." 🥖

Konobar donosi kavu i kruh. Ana pije kavu. Kava je topla i dobra. 😊

Ana pita: "Koliko je to?"

Konobar kaže: "Pet eura."

Ana daje novac. "Izvolite!"

"Hvala! Doviđenja!" kaže konobar.

"Doviđenja!" kaže Ana. Ana je sretna. Jutro je lijepo. ☀️

## English translation 🇬🇧

I am Ana. I am in a café. ☕

The waiter says: "Good morning! What would you like?"

Ana says: "Good morning! A coffee, please."

"Would you like milk?" the waiter asks.

"Yes, milk, please," says Ana. "And bread, please." 🥖

The waiter brings the coffee and bread. Ana drinks the coffee. The coffee is hot and good. 😊

Ana asks: "How much is that?"

The waiter says: "Five euros."

Ana gives the money. "Here you go!"

"Thank you! Goodbye!" says the waiter.

"Goodbye!" says Ana. Ana is happy. The morning is lovely. ☀️

## Useful Croatian patterns 🧩

**1. Ordering with the accusative: *Kavu, molim.***

- Meaning: "A coffee, please."
- The dictionary form is **kava** (feminine, ends in **-a**). When a feminine noun is the thing you want, receive, or drink, **-a** changes to **-u**. This is the accusative case, used for direct objects.
- Examples: *Pijem kavu.* (I drink coffee.) *Želim vodu.* (I want water.) *Imam knjigu.* (I have a book.)
- Masculine inanimate nouns such as **kruh** do not change in the accusative: *Želim kruh.* (I want bread.)

**2. Polite requests: *molim* and *izvolite***

- **Molim** means "please" when you ask for something. **Izvolite** means "here you go" or "please, go ahead" when you hand something over.
- Examples: *Vodu, molim.* (Water, please.) *Izvolite kavu.* (Here is your coffee.)

**3. Asking questions with *Što* and *Koliko***

- **Što želite?** means "What would you like?" **Koliko je to?** means "How much is that?"
- Examples: *Što pijete?* (What are you drinking?) *Koliko je kava?* (How much is the coffee?)
- The formal **vi** form ends in **-te**: *želite, pijete*. Use it with strangers, such as a waiter.

**4. Yes/no questions with *li***

- **Želite li mlijeko?** means "Would you like milk?" Adding **li** after the verb turns a statement into a yes/no question.
- Examples: *Pijete li kavu?* (Do you drink coffee?) *Imate li kruh?* (Do you have bread?)

**5. Adjectives agree with their noun: *Kava je topla.***

- **Topla** is feminine because **kava** is feminine. The masculine form is **topao**, and the neuter is **toplo**.
- Examples: *Kava je dobra.* (The coffee is good.) *Kruh je dobar.* (The bread is good.) *Jutro je lijepo.* (The morning is lovely.)

## Quick comprehension check 🎯

**1. Što pije Ana? / What does Ana drink?**

- a) Vodu / Water
- b) Kavu / Coffee
- c) Sok / Juice

**2. Koliko je to? / How much is it?**

- a) Pet eura / Five euros
- b) Deset eura / Ten euros
- c) Tri eura / Three euros

**3. Što znači "Izvolite!"? / What does "Izvolite!" mean?**

- a) Doviđenja / Goodbye
- b) Hvala / Thank you
- c) Evo, izvolite / Here you go

## Answer key

1. **b**: Ana pije kavu.
2. **a**: Konobar kaže: "Pet eura."
3. **c**: Ana daje novac i kaže "Izvolite!"
