# Šetnja sa psom 🐕🇭🇷
*A Walk with the Dog*

## Croatian story 🇭🇷

Danas je topao dan. ☀️

Marko šeta sa psom. 🐕 Pas je Bruno. Bruno je velik i sretan.

Marko i Bruno idu u park. 🌳 Bruno voli park.

Marko ima loptu. 🎾

"Bruno, lopta!" kaže Marko.

Marko baca loptu. Bruno trči i donosi loptu.

"Bravo, Bruno!" kaže Marko.

Ana je u parku. Ana šeta sa psom. 🐩 Pas je Lola.

"Bok, Ana!" kaže Marko.

"Bok, Marko!" kaže Ana.

Bruno i Lola trče. Marko šeta sa Anom.

Marko i Ana sjede u parku. Bruno i Lola piju vodu. 💧

Marko i Ana su sretni. Dan je lijep. 😊

## English translation 🇬🇧

Today is a warm day. ☀️

Marko is walking with the dog. 🐕 The dog is Bruno. Bruno is big and happy.

Marko and Bruno go to the park. 🌳 Bruno loves the park.

Marko has a ball. 🎾

"Bruno, ball!" says Marko.

Marko throws the ball. Bruno runs and brings the ball.

"Bravo, Bruno!" says Marko.

Ana is in the park. Ana is walking with a dog. 🐩 The dog is Lola.

"Hi, Ana!" says Marko.

"Hi, Marko!" says Ana.

Bruno and Lola run. Marko walks with Ana.

Marko and Ana sit in the park. Bruno and Lola drink water. 💧

Marko and Ana are happy. The day is lovely. 😊

## Useful Croatian patterns 🧩

**1. Verbs in *-ati*: *šetati***
- **šetati** (to walk, to stroll): *šetam, šetaš, šeta, šetamo, šetate, šetaju*. The noun is **šetnja** (a walk).
- Many **-ati** verbs follow this pattern (*gledati, imati*), but not all: **trčati** has *trčim, trčiš, trči, ... trče*. Learn the "he/she" form with each new verb.
- Examples: *Šetam sa psom.* (I walk with the dog.) *Ana gleda park.* (Ana looks at the park.) *Marko ima loptu.* (Marko has a ball.)

**2. "With": *s* or *sa* + instrumental, *sa psom***
- **s** and **sa** both mean "with". **Sa** is often used to make pronunciation easier, for example before **s, š, z, ž** or a cluster of consonants.
- The noun takes **-om**: **pas → psom** (the **a** drops out), **Marko → Markom**, **Ana → Anom**, **mlijeko → mlijekom**.
- Examples: *Marko šeta sa Anom.* (Marko walks with Ana.) *Kava s mlijekom.* (Coffee with milk.) *Idem u grad s Petrom.* (I'm going to town with Petar.)

**3. Liking things: *voljeti***
- **voljeti**: *volim, voliš, voli, volimo, volite, vole*. The thing you like is in the accusative: feminine **-a → -u**, masculine things stay the same.
- Examples: *Bruno voli park.* (Bruno loves the park.) *Bruno voli loptu.* (Bruno loves the ball.) *Volim kavu.* (I love coffee.)
- For people and animals, masculine nouns change in the accusative (*volim Marka*). Learn that later.

**4. Going and being: *u park*, *u parku***
- **Movement**: *Marko i Bruno idu u park.* (accusative). **Location**: *Marko i Ana sjede u parku.* (locative).
- For more than one person, "he/she" forms change: **ide → idu**, **trči → trče**, **pije → piju**.

**5. More than one: *su sretni***
- Singular: **sretan** (m), **sretna** (f), **sretno** (n). Plural: **sretni** (m or a mixed group), **sretne** (all feminine). The verb is **su**.
- Examples: *Marko i Ana su sretni.* (Marko and Ana are happy.) *Ana i Lola su sretne.* (Ana and Lola are happy, all feminine.) *Psi su veliki.* (The dogs are big.)

## Quick comprehension check 🎯

**1. Tko je Bruno? / Who is Bruno?**
- a) Prijatelj / A friend
- b) Pas / A dog
- c) Šef / The boss

**2. Što Marko ima? / What does Marko have?**
- a) Kartu / A ticket
- b) Kolač / A cake
- c) Loptu / A ball

**3. Što znači "Bruno voli park"? / What does "Bruno voli park" mean?**
- a) Bruno is in the park / Bruno is in the park
- b) Bruno loves the park / Bruno loves the park
- c) Bruno goes to the park / Bruno goes to the park

## Answer key

1. **b**: Pas je Bruno.
2. **c**: Marko ima loptu.
3. **b**: "Bruno voli park" znači "Bruno loves the park."
